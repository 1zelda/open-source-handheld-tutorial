### 1.1 F1（TMMN）——本轮最强来源，逐武器关键原文

这是**针对 MM1 NES 本体**的武器分析（不是粉丝引擎重制），8 项齐全。以下逐字引用（快照行号指 `tmmn_weapons_mm1_nes_2014-03-17.md`）：

- **Mega Buster**（L56–82）：`Uses With a Full Charge: Infinite`；`Number on Screen: 3`；`Path: Straight to edge of screen.`；`Passes Through Walls: Yes`；`Piercing: No`；`its shot is relatively weak and the projectile is tiny`；`fragile little egg-shaped pellets`。
- **Rolling Cutter**（L84–119）：`Color Scheme: White/Grey`；`Weapon Type: Blade, Boomerang`；`Piercing: Yes`；`Passes Through Walls: Yes`；`Uses With a Full Charge: 28`；`Number on Screen: 1`；`Path: Rounded return. - **Thunder Beam**（L121–155）：`Color Scheme: Yellow/Grey`；`Secondary Benefits: Terrain Clearance`；`Uses With a Full Charge: 28`；`Number on Screen: 1 use, 3 beams`；`Path: Winds straight ahead in a tall, irregular pattern, simultaneously straight up and straight down in shorter, tighter patterns.`；`one usage of the weapon deducts one bar of ammunition`；`it usurps the Super Arm's only job by clearing out Guts blocks`；`the infamous "pause glitch"`；`activating the Thunder Beam on a busy screen introduces slowdown`；Elec Man 自己的版本 `does not destroy those blocks in his room`。
- **Ice Slasher**（L157–192）：`Color Scheme: White/Violet`；`Secondary Benefits: Freezes enemies and fire traps`；`Uses With a Full Charge: 28`；`Number on Screen: 1`；`Path: Straight to edge of screen.`；`Another 1:1 ratio here`；`they don't deal damage to anything apart from Fire Man`；`Its ability to freeze the traps in Fire Man's stage– and later Wily's Castle`；`It can be helpful to stop the respawning of enemies, such as the Killer Bullets and Flying Shells`；Ice Man 版本可多发、有伤害、空中释放会停住上升/下降（`it does not carry over` 到玩家版）。

- **Hyper Bomb**（L231–266）：`Color Scheme: White/Lime`；`Piercing: No`；`Passes Through Walls: No (though the blast does)`；`Uses With a Full Charge: 14`；`Number on Screen: 1`；`Path: Tall arc, bounce followed by shorter arc, rest. - **Super Arm**（L268–306）：`Color Scheme: White/Brown`；`Weapon Type: Close-range projectile`；`Piercing: Yes`；`Passes Through Walls: No, but shards do`；`Uses With a Full Charge: 14`；`Number on Screen: 1`；`Path: Block arcs a short distance into the air and forward, dealing damage along the way. - **Magnet Beam**（L308–332）：`Color Scheme: Standard`（指**角色配色不变**，不是光束颜色）；`Utility Type: Stationary Platform Generator`；`Passes Through Walls: No`；`Uses With a Full Charge: 14`；`Number on Screen: 5`；`Path: Straight ahead. ### 1.4 F4–F10（MegaManMaker Wiki）——粉丝引擎陷阱，**只取定性身份与 MM1 素材分布**

⚠️ 这 7 页是 **Mega Man Maker（粉丝引擎）** 的资产文档。`Game information` 里的 damage/energy/秒数/ tile 数是 **Maker 实现值，不是 MM1 原作数值**，一律不导入为 MM1 事实。可采纳的只有：

1. **武器身份定性**（谁是哪个 Boss 的武器、回旋/冰冻/爆炸/护盾/举块/平台这些类别特征）——与 TMMN 一致。
2. **MM1 素材分布声明**（F4 Super Arm 页逐字）：`a green one from Cut Man's stage, a yellow one from Elec Man's stage, a brown one from Guts Man's stage, a gray one from the first Wily stage, and a dark green one from the third Wily stage.`（**5 色 Guts block 分布**，Super Arm 专用 base 选景依据）。
3. **明确排除的非 MM1 机制**（F9 Hyper Bomb raw 的 Trivia 逐字）：
   - `The ability to control Hyper Bomb's trajectory is based off of Bomb Man's version of the weapon in Mega Man Powered Up.`（上下键控制轨迹 = Powered Up，**不是 MM1**）
   - `The plus-shaped explosion is a reference to the Bomberman series`（**十字形爆炸 = Bomberman 致敬，不是 MM1**；MM1 是 TMMN 说的 `tight radius`）
   - F9 正文 `exploding after 1 second` / `explodes upon contact` / `6 tiles` 均为 Maker 值；MM1 玩家版是**静止后延时爆炸、非接触即爆**（TMMN 全文 + Bomb Man 版才接触爆）。
4. **Maker 数值留档作对照（不当 MM1 事实）**：F8 Ice Slasher `freeze ... for four seconds`（MM1 冰冻时长未知，须探针）；F10 Fire Storm `shield for 1 second` / `hit a target up to three times`（MM1 护盾时长/命中数未知，须探针）；F7 Rolling Cutter `travels 6 tiles in a tear-drop trajectory`（MM1 实际弧线须探针）。
