'''Ice Slasher''' is [[Ice Man]]'s signature [[weapon]] originating from ''[[Mega Man 1]]'' and reappearing in ''[[Mega Man: Dr. Wily's Revenge]]'', ''[[Mega Man: The Wily Wars]]'' and ''[[Mega Man Powered Up]]''. It is featured in ''[[Mega Man Maker]]'' as of version [[1.1.0]].

It sends out a fast projectile that deals no damage, but will freeze any enemy it touches for four seconds. This effect remains even if the player switches weapons. It can pass through shields, terrain, and objects as well. Only one Ice Slasher can be onscreen at a time.

Destructible objects such as [[Dust Block]]s can still be broken by Ice Slasher. As it is an ice weapon, [[Flame Pillar]]s, [[Fire Wall]]s, [[lava]], and other fire assets affected by ice can be frozen by it too, making them safe to touch. [[Crazy Razy]] will be instantly defeated by it instead of being frozen. If [[Bounce Man]] is hit by this weapon set as a weakness, Ice Slasher will freeze his parts in place temporarily.

== Trivia ==
* Ice Slasher has the ability to pierce certain Robot Master Shields even if it's not set as their weakness; those Robot Masters are [[Skull Man]] and [[Pump Man]].
* Ice Slasher's ingame icon was redone with a darker blue background and a shadow effect behind the projectile in ''Mega Man Maker'' version [[1.8.4]].
