'''Rolling Cutter''' is [[Cut Man]]'s signature [[Weapons|weapon]], originating from ''[[Mega Man 1]]'' and reappearing in ''[[Mega Man: Dr. Wily's Revenge]]'', ''[[Mega Man: The Wily Wars]]'' and ''[[Mega Man Powered Up]]''. It is featured in ''[[Mega Man Maker]]'' as of version [[1.0.0]].

When the attack key is pressed, the player throws a boomerang-like cutter that travels 6 tiles in a tear-drop trajectory before returning to the player. If the player moves while the cutter comes back, the cutter will follow them. It can also pass through walls and destroy the entire [[Spine]] enemies (except the [[Electric Gabyoall]]) in one hit. Because of its speed it is possible to run away from the rolling cutter blade and have it follow you through the level.
