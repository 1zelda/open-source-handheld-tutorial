'_Thunder Beam_ is Elec Man's signature weapon, originating from _[Mega Man 1](/index.php/Mega_Man_1 "Mega Man 1")_ and reappearing in _[Mega Man: Dr. Wily's Revenge](/index.php/Mega_Man:_Dr._Wily%27s_Revenge "Mega Man: Dr. Wily's Revenge")_, _[Mega Man: The Wily Wars](/index.php/Mega_Man:_The_Wily_Wars "Mega Man: The Wily Wars")_ and _[Mega Man Powered Up](/index.php/Mega_Man_Powered_Up "Mega Man Powered Up")_. It is featured in _[Mega Man Maker](/index.php/Mega_Man_Maker "Mega Man Maker")_ as of version [1.0.0](/index.php/1.0.0 "1.0.0").

It lets the user shoot three slow electric beams: one that goes straight forward, and two that go up and down. These beams pass through all enemies and terrain, and can destroy [Super Arm Blocks](/index.php/Super_Arm_Block "Super Arm Block") both set by the level object and the weapon [Super Arm](/index.php/Super_Arm "Super Arm"). If fired point-blank against an enemy, all three beams will deal damage together. Since it's an electric-based weapon, it can power Fuse Crossers/Xtenders, deactivate Fire Met Spawners, and trigger [Spring Man](/index.php/Spring_Man "Spring Man")'s electromagnetism.
